For my friend, my sister, my mentor.

Utso,

I've wanted to make something for you for a while, and no ordinary message felt big enough. So here it is: a small garden, just for you.

We've been friends for a year and a half now, and somewhere along the way you stopped being just a friend. You became the person I trust, the person I go to when things get confusing, the sister I got to choose.

You're also the best mentor I've ever had. You push me to think better and do better, and you do it with patience, even when I don't make it easy. A lot of what I'm getting right, I'm getting right because of you.

I picked lilies because they're your favorite, and because they bloom quietly and make the whole place brighter without asking for credit. That felt like you. Brown and green, because that's you too: steady, grounded, always growing.

Thank you for being there. Thank you for being you.
